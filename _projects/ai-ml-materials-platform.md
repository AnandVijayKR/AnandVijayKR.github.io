**2023 – Present**

Building GUI-based software tools combining transformer-based language models with physics-based simulation for polymer and composite materials property prediction, inverse design, and LLM-assisted FEA workflow automation.

**Scope**
- Transformer language model architectures for materials science prediction
- Physics-informed surrogate modeling integrated with RVE micromechanics
- Inverse-design workflows for property targets
- LLM-assisted FEA pre-processing and post-processing automation
- GUI front-end for non-CAE users

**Status:** Active. Initial transformer architecture benchmarked against high-fidelity RVE outputs; expanding training data and use cases.
