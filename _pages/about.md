Research Engineer with **10+ years** delivering production-ready simulation solutions for aerospace and defense programs — including collaborations with Spirit AeroSystems, NASA, KART, and multiple SBIR initiatives.

I specialize in translating complex, multi-physics and multi-scale models into validated, decision-ready engineering tools. My work spans first-principles FEA to data-driven AI/ML platforms — bridging simulation and experiment to build as-built, physics-grounded models for design, manufacturing, and performance optimization.

**Current research focus:**
- **Digital Twin development** — combining physics-based simulation with real-time data for predictive modeling and lifecycle-aware decision-making
- **AI/ML-enabled engineering tools** — physics-informed frameworks, GUI platforms for materials prediction, and LLM-assisted FEA workflow automation
- **Thermomechanical process simulation** — high-fidelity coupled thermal–mechanical FEA of solid-state additive manufacturing, residual stress prediction, and experimental validation

I welcome research collaboration in computational mechanics, digital twin development, and AI/ML-enabled engineering. Please reach out via email.
