**2014 – 2017**

Investigated forming and creep behavior of honeycomb sandwich structures using advanced nonlinear FEA (MSC Marc, HyperMesh). Extended analyses to micro–macro multiscale frameworks with custom FORTRAN subroutines for anisotropic hyperelastic creep behavior.

**Scope**
- Nonlinear FEA of honeycomb forming under spherical, cylindrical, and multi-radius bending
- Micro–macro multiscale modeling of woven composite honeycomb cores
- Custom FORTRAN subroutines for anisotropic hyperelastic creep
- CAD modeling (CATIA V5, SolidWorks) supporting simulation-driven studies
- Analytical microstructure formulations supporting localized response capture

**Status:** Delivered. Foundation for later defect-aware composite analysis and digital twin work.
