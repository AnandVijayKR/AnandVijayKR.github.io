**2023 – Present** · *Active collaboration with NIAR*

Developing end-to-end digital twin infrastructure integrating physics-based process simulation, real-time sensor data, and validation workflows for advanced manufacturing programs.

**Scope**
- Boundary-condition identification from sensor and metrology data
- Model calibration against in-situ measurements
- Virtual process qualification for production-line decisions
- Sequential thermal–mechanical coupling architecture
- Parameterized HPC job management for cluster-scale execution

**Status:** Active. Architecture implemented and validated against thermocouple measurements; extending to new geometries and process windows.
