---

### Digital Twin Framework for Advanced Manufacturing &nbsp;`2023 – Present`

> Developing end-to-end digital twin infrastructure integrating physics-based process simulation, real-time sensor data, and validation workflows for advanced manufacturing programs. Includes boundary-condition identification, model calibration, and virtual process qualification in collaboration with NIAR.

---

### AI/ML-Enabled Materials & Process Prediction Platform &nbsp;`2023 – Present`

> Building GUI-based software tools combining transformer-based language models with physics-based simulation for polymer and composite materials property prediction, inverse design, and LLM-assisted FEA automation. Integrates RVE micromechanics with data-driven surrogate modeling.

---

### Thermomechanical Process Simulation — Solid-State Manufacturing &nbsp;`2022 – Present`

> High-fidelity coupled thermal–mechanical FEA of solid-state additive manufacturing processes. Encompasses heat generation modeling, multi-layer deposition sequencing, residual stress prediction, and experimental thermocouple validation. Automated end-to-end simulation pipeline development.

---

### Defect-Aware Composite Structural Analysis — Aerospace Structures &nbsp;`2020 – 2022`

> Delivered global-to-local aircraft wing simulation frameworks incorporating manufacturing distortion, XFEM-based fatigue crack growth, CDM progressive damage, and CFD-driven thermal loading. Integrated defect characterization directly into structural performance models for aerospace qualification programs.

---

### Multiscale RVE Micromechanics & Digital Material Generation &nbsp;`2019 – Present`

> Developed digital material generation toolkits for composite laminates — predicting ply-level elastic, strength, and thermal properties using RVE-based micromechanics, including explicit defect effects (voids, fiber waviness, ply misalignment).

---

### Composite Fabrication & Experimental Characterization &nbsp;`2014 – 2022`

> Designed and executed experimental programs covering panel fabrication (layup, cure, debulk), mechanical and thermal testing (fracture, fatigue, SBS, DSC, CTE, stress relaxation), and microscopy-based defect characterization. Experimental outputs directly informed and validated computational models.

---

### Nonlinear FEA & Multiscale Modeling — Honeycomb Core Structures &nbsp;`2014 – 2017`

> Investigated forming and creep behavior of honeycomb sandwich structures using advanced nonlinear FEA (MSC Marc, HyperMesh). Extended analyses to micro–macro multiscale frameworks with custom FORTRAN subroutines for anisotropic hyperelastic creep behavior.
