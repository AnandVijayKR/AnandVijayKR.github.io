**2022 – Present**

High-fidelity coupled thermal–mechanical finite element analysis of solid-state additive manufacturing processes — covering heat generation modeling, multi-layer deposition sequencing, residual stress prediction, and experimental validation.

**Scope**
- Heat generation modeling with spatially distributed source terms
- Path-synchronized element activation for multi-layer deposition sequencing
- Residual stress and distortion prediction with sequential coupling
- Experimental validation against thermocouple measurements
- End-to-end automated simulation pipeline (CAD → mesh → solver → post-processing)

**Status:** Active. Residual stress prediction workflow validated for multi-layer AFSD deposition; generalization to irregular geometries in progress.
