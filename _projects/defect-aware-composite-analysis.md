**2020 – 2022**

Delivered global-to-local aircraft wing simulation frameworks incorporating manufacturing distortion, XFEM-based fatigue crack growth, CDM progressive damage, and CFD-driven thermal loading. Integrated defect characterization directly into structural performance models for aerospace qualification programs.

**Scope**
- Global-to-local sub-modeling for aircraft wing components
- XFEM-based fatigue crack growth with defect-informed initiation
- CDM progressive damage for composite laminates
- CFD-driven thermal loading on structural models
- Manufacturing distortion mapped onto as-built geometry for performance prediction

**Status:** Delivered. Framework supported aerospace qualification programs and informed downstream digital twin work.
