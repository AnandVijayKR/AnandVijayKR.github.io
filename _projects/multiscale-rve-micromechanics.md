**2019 – Present**

Developed digital material generation toolkits for composite laminates — predicting ply-level elastic, strength, and thermal properties using RVE-based micromechanics, with explicit treatment of manufacturing-induced defects.

**Scope**
- Representative Volume Element (RVE) generation for fiber-reinforced composites
- Predicting ply-level elastic, strength, and thermal properties
- Explicit modeling of voids, fiber waviness, and ply misalignment
- Linkage from micro-scale defect distributions to macro-scale structural performance
- Toolkit integration into broader simulation pipelines

**Status:** Active. Toolkit forms the materials backbone for digital twin and defect-aware structural analysis work.
